Physical tells

Each opponent picks a little movement at the start of every game — a twitch, a sway, a flicker — and keeps it. Work out what sets it off.

Who you are up against

    How to play

    1. Texas hold'em, five seats, 20 coins each. Blinds are 1 and 2, and the dealer button moves one seat clockwise every hand.
    2. Two cards each, then betting on the flop, the turn and the river. Fold, check, call, raise or shove.
    3. Best five out of your two plus the five on the table wins the pot. Equal hands split it.
    4. Run out of coins and you are out. Take everyone else's and the table is yours.
